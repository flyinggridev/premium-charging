Good to know

Questions, answered.

Some details depend on the property, electricity connection and partnership agreement.

Does listing my land cost anything?

Submitting an enquiry is free. Any site assessment, installation, electrical work or other project costs must be confirmed in the written proposal before you proceed.

How much can I earn?

There is no universal payout. It depends on site traffic, number and type of chargers, utilisation, electricity tariff and the commercial model. Calculator figures are illustrative only.

Which properties can apply?

Malls, hotels, cafés, commercial plots, parking lots, highway sites, fleet depots, offices and other locations with suitable vehicle access may apply.

Which party pays the electricity bill?

This must be agreed for each project. The written proposal should clarify the meter, electricity tariff, bill payment and how revenue share is calculated.

How soon can a charger be installed?

Timelines depend on site approval, power availability, equipment, permits and installation work. A timeline should be provided only after the site review.

Will Flying Grid guarantee monthly income?

No income is guaranteed by this website. Fixed rent applies only if agreed in a signed contract; revenue-share payouts depend on actual charging activity and contract terms.
